## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### Dashboard 1 — Executive Overview
**Purpose:** High-level tournament and team performance summary for FIFA leadership and tournament directors.

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Tournaments (19) · Total Goals (2,208) · Matches Played (772) · Avg Goals/Match (2.860) |
| Charts | Avg Goals/Match trend (1930–2014) · Tournament Attendance bar · Top 15 Teams by Wins · Win Rate vs Scoring Rate scatter |
| Filters | Era dropdown · Year range slider · Titles Won colour legend |
| Tab Name | `Executive Overview` |

---

### Dashboard 2 — Match Analytics
**Purpose:** Match-level outcome and scoring pattern analysis for analysts and broadcast scheduling teams.

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Home Win Rate (58.66%) · Away Win Rate (18.90%) · Draw Rate (22.44%) · Goalless Rate (8.27%) |
| Charts | Match Outcome pie · Goals Distribution histogram · How Matches Were Decided bar · Avg Goals by Stage · Attendance vs Goals scatter |
| Filters | Win Type · Stage · Era · Is Knockout slider · Year range slider |
| Tab Name | `Match Analytics` |

---

### Dashboard 3 — Player & Team Deep Dive
**Purpose:** Player career and team dominance analysis for coaches, scouts, and federation analytics teams.

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Most Goals (16 — Klose) · Most Appearances (32 — Ronaldo) · Total Players (7,638) · Players Scored (1,177) |
| Charts | Top 15 All-Time Goal Scorers · Top 15 Teams by Goal Difference · Discipline Record (Cards) · Appearances vs Goals quadrant scatter |
| Filters | Team selector · Tournaments Attended · Goal Difference Label · Team dropdown |
| Tab Name | `Player & Team Deep Dive` |
